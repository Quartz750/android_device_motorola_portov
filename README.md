Device configuration for Motorola moto g 67 power 5G
=========================================

The Motorola moto g 67 power 5G (codenamed _"portov"_) is a mid-range smartphone from Motorola.

It was globally released in November 2025.

## Device specifications

Basic   | Spec Sheet
-------:|:-------------------------
Chipset | Snapdragon® 7s Gen 2 (SM7435-AB)
CPU | 4x Arm Cortex-A78 (Up to 2.4GHz) and 4x Arm Cortex-A55 (Up to 1.95GHz)
GPU | Adreno 710 (Up to 940MHz)
RAM | 8/12GB (LPDDR4X)
ROM | 128/256/512GB (UFS2.2)
Shipped Android Version | 15
Battery | Non-removable, 7000 mAh
Display | 6.7″, 30/60/90/120Hz, 1080x2400px (391 ppi), low blue light LCD, 1050nits peak brightness
Rear camera | Sony LYT600 (50MP wide, 8MP 118° ultrawide)
Front camera | 32MP in-display
